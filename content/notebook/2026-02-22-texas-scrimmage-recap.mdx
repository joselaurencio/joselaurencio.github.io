Ran the season robot at the Adventist Robotics Texas Scrimmage today — the
first time it's been on a field with other teams instead of just practice
space. Drivetrain and scoring mechanism both held up fine across matches.

The one surprise: autonomous drifted off its intended path on this field in
a way it never did in practice. Practice space doesn't have the same tile
seams as the actual competition field, and that seam is apparently enough to
throw off dead-reckoning-based movement. Noting this down before champs so
it doesn't get lost — this is exactly the kind of thing that's obvious in
hindsight and easy to forget under event-week pressure.
