Adventist Robotics Championship results: Dean's List Semifinalist, Inspire
Award (1st), and Finalist Award (2nd) — the best season result on record for
Team #24813.

The autonomous fix from the Texas Scrimmage held up on the championship
field, which was the thing I was most nervous about going in. The judged
interview for the Inspire Award asked directly about that scrimmage-to-champs
fix — glad it was written down instead of half-remembered under pressure.

Biggest takeaway going into next season: the judged awards reward how well
you can explain the process, including what went wrong, not just what the
robot does on the field.
