First real piece of the new portfolio is live: the Mission Control homepage.

Went spec-first on this one — a full PRD, a design system with every color
pairing checked against the actual WCAG contrast formula, and a complete file
architecture, all written before any component code. That process caught two
real mistakes before they shipped: a text color that looked fine but failed
accessibility contrast, and a YouTube channel ID that pointed at the wrong
channel entirely.

Next up: the pages the homepage already links to — Projects, Notebook,
Robotics, and the rest.
